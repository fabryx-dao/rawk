Deepself

Give your agent a face.

---

Create precise AI personas that sit between your agent and the language model. Powerful, dynamic, plug-and-play identities your agent can spin up and deploy for specific tasks.

Create personas for:
- Life coaching and reflection
- Domain expertise (legal, medical, creative)
- Different perspectives on the same problem

Train them with:
- Documents you identify with
- Conversations with other personas
- Your own writing and values

Each persona builds its own knowledge graph. Your agent selects the right face for the job.
